AI automation for small businesses

Every call answered. Every follow-up sent. Every booking filled — while you do the work only you can do.

Small businesses don’t lose jobs to competitors. They lose them to missed calls, slow follow-ups, and empty slots in the calendar. AgenticBiz installs AI that handles the busywork — answering, booking, reminding, following up — so no job slips through while you’re on the tools, with clients, or off the clock.

Where the work leaks

It’s almost never the work itself. It’s the work around the work — the phone ringing while you’re mid-job, the quote that never got chased, the customer who booked elsewhere because nobody called back. That’s what the AI takes over.

Missed calls

A customer who reaches voicemail calls the next business on the list. Every unanswered ring is a job walking away.

Slow follow-up

Quotes, questions, and leads that sit unanswered overnight go cold. Speed is the whole sale in a local market.

Empty slots

Cancellations nobody re-fills, reminders nobody sends, no-shows nobody chases. The calendar leaks money every week.

What it does for your business

Answers

Calls, texts, and messages answered around the clock, in your voice, with your prices and hours. Real questions get real answers; anything it can’t handle goes straight to you.

Books

Appointments booked, confirmed, rescheduled, and reminded — straight into your calendar. Cancellations get offered to the waitlist before the slot goes cold.

Follows up

Quotes chased, leads nurtured, past customers invited back, reviews requested after the job. The follow-up you mean to do, done every time.

Keeps the books moving

Invoices sent, payments nudged, receipts filed. The admin tail that eats your evenings, handled in the background.

Built for businesses like yours

Salons and clinics. Contractors and trades. Shops, cafés, and restaurants. Accountants, lawyers, and local services. If the phone ringing is money, and the owner is also the workforce, this is built for you — no IT team, no software project, no learning curve. We set it up; you run your business.

You stay in charge
